Study Introduction and Demo

This script will be used before the formal study begins. The goal is to let participants understand and briefly try the tool without turning the introduction into a long tutorial.
Introduction Script

Thank you for participating. In this study, you will use a reading interface with sign language video support. The page shows written text on the left and a sign language support tool on the right.

The tool lets you choose how sign videos are shown for the text. You can change four settings:

Location controls where the video appears. Popup shows the video near the selected text. Side shows the video in the right panel.

Video Segmentation controls the size of the video unit. You can view signs by word, sentence, paragraph, or full text.

Linking Granularity controls what parts of the text can be selected. For example, if linking is set to word, individual words may be clickable. If it is set to sentence, whole sentences may be selectable.

Navigation controls how the text and video interact. Interactive Text lets text selections control the video. Interactive Video lets the video guide the highlighted text. Both allow interaction in both directions. Some navigation options may be disabled when they are not needed or not valid for the selected settings.

Before we begin the formal task, please take a minute or two to try the tool freely.

Quick Demo

1. Open the sign language support menu on the right side of the page.

2. Try ‘Location’.
Select ‘Popup’, then click a piece of text. Notice that the video appears near the text. Then try ‘Side’ and notice that the video appears in the side panel.

3. Try ‘Video Segmentation’.
  Select ‘Sentence’, then click a sentence in the text. The sentence video should appear.

4. Try ‘Linking Granularity’.
With video segmentation set to ‘Sentence’, select linking granularity ‘Word’. Now click a word inside a sentence. The tool should show the sentence video and navigate within that sentence, not open a separate word-only video.

5. Try ‘Navigation’.
With ‘Sentence’ video segmentation and ‘Word’ linking granularity, choose ‘Interactive Text’. Click different words and notice how the video changes. Then try ‘Both’ and scroll the text to see how the video and highlight can stay connected.

6. Try a same-level setting.
Select ‘Sentence’ for both video segmentation and linking granularity. Navigation should be disabled, but clicking a sentence should still show its video.

7. Try an invalid combination.
Select ‘Full Text’ video segmentation and ‘Paragraph’ linking granularity. Then change video segmentation to ‘Sentence’. The tool should automatically disable invalid linking choices and fall back to a valid one.

Transition to Study

That is the end of the practice demo. During the study, please use the tool naturally. You may change settings whenever you want. We are interested in how the tool supports your reading and understanding, so there are no right or wrong settings to choose.
